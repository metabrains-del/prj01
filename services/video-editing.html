How It Works

Our Process

From raw footage to final export — a pipeline built for retention.

01

Footage Review & Brief

You upload your raw footage and fill out a brief covering tone, pacing, reference videos, and any must-include moments. We review everything before touching the timeline — no wasted cuts.

02

Rough Cut

We build the structural edit first — pacing, story arc, and talking head cuts. For YouTube, we apply retention-optimised pacing: pattern interrupts every 60–90 seconds, strategic B-roll placement, and tight audio sync.

03

Motion Graphics & Sound Design

Custom lower thirds, animated text, transitions, and kinetic typography are added. Sound design includes music selection, SFX layering, and audio levelling. For short-form, we add captions with animated word-by-word reveals.

04

Color Grading

Every video gets a professional colour grade — not just a LUT slap. We correct exposure, match shots, and apply a cinematic grade that fits your brand aesthetic. Skin tones are always prioritised.

05

Review & Final Export

You review the edit and request changes. Final export is delivered in platform-optimised specs: 4K H.264 for YouTube, 1080×1920 for Reels/TikTok, and any additional formats you need. Turnaround: 3–7 business days.
