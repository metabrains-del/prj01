How It Works

Our Process

Every script goes through a battle-tested pipeline built for performance.

01

Discovery Call

We start with a 30-minute deep-dive into your brand, audience, tone, and goals. We study your existing content, competitors, and what's already working in your niche. No generic templates — we build a custom voice profile for you.

02

Research & Ideation

Our writers research the topic thoroughly — pulling data, trending angles, and audience pain points. We identify the strongest hook angle before writing a single word. For YouTube, we also analyse retention patterns on similar videos.

03

First Draft

The script is written with platform-specific pacing in mind. YouTube long-form scripts include a hook (0–30s), retention loops, and a strong CTA. Short-form hooks are engineered to stop the scroll in the first 2 seconds. Ad scripts are built around a single conversion action.

04

Revision & Refinement

You get 2 rounds of revisions included. We don't just accept feedback — we push back when something won't perform. Our goal is the best possible script, not just the one you asked for.

05

Final Delivery

Delivered as a formatted Google Doc with speaker notes, on-screen text cues, and B-roll suggestions. For ad scripts, we include multiple hook variations to A/B test. Turnaround: 3–5 business days.
