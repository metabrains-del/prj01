How It Works

The Full Pipeline

From zero to published — every step handled, every detail owned.

01

Onboarding & Strategy Session

We start with a deep-dive strategy session — your brand, audience, goals, tone, and content pillars. We audit your existing channel or social presence, map your competitors, and build a 30-day content roadmap before a single frame is shot. You get a dedicated production manager as your single point of contact for the entire engagement.

02

Topic Research & Script

Our strategists identify the highest-opportunity topics based on search volume, trending angles, and your niche gaps. Our writers then craft a full script — hook engineered for the first 3 seconds, retention loops built in, and a CTA that converts. You review and approve before anything goes to production.

03

Recording Guidance

You record using our shot list and teleprompter-ready script. We provide a pre-shoot checklist covering lighting, audio, framing, and background setup so your footage is edit-ready. For clients on premium retainers, we offer remote directing via live video call during your recording session.

04

Full Post-Production

Our editors take your raw footage through the complete post pipeline: rough cut, motion graphics, custom lower thirds, B-roll sourcing, sound design, music licensing, animated captions, and a full cinematic colour grade. For YouTube, we apply retention-optimised pacing with pattern interrupts every 60–90 seconds. Short-form cuts are produced simultaneously.

05

Thumbnail & SEO Package

Every video gets a custom thumbnail designed for maximum CTR — tested against our internal benchmarks. We also write the SEO-optimised title, description, tags, and chapter markers. For YouTube, we include a pinned comment template and community post copy to drive early engagement signals.

06

Publish, Review & Iterate

We deliver all assets ready to upload. On retainer plans, we review your analytics 7 days post-publish — CTR, average view duration, and audience retention curves. We use that data to sharpen the next video's hook, pacing, and thumbnail. Every month, the content gets smarter.
