How It Works

Our Process

Strategy without data is just guessing. We don't guess.

01

Channel Audit

We pull your analytics and do a full forensic audit — CTR, average view duration, audience retention curves, top-performing content, and drop-off points. We identify exactly what's working, what's killing your growth, and what your algorithm is rewarding.

02

Niche & Competitor Research

We map your niche landscape — who's winning, what topics are trending, what gaps exist, and where your unfair advantage lies. We use YouTube search data, trending tools, and manual analysis to find content opportunities your competitors are missing.

03

Positioning & Pillar Strategy

We define your content pillars — the 3–5 core topic areas that will anchor your channel. Each pillar is chosen based on search volume, audience demand, and your ability to own it. We also define your unique positioning so you're not just another creator in the space.

04

Content Calendar Build

We build a 30–90 day content calendar with specific video ideas, titles, thumbnail concepts, and publish dates. Every idea is mapped to a pillar, a search intent, and a growth goal. No filler content — every video has a purpose.

05

Monthly Growth Reviews

On retainer plans, we review your analytics monthly, adjust the strategy based on what's performing, and update the calendar. Growth is iterative — we stay in the loop and keep optimising.
