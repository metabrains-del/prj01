The Brief

A product launch with
one shot to get it right.

Flowmatic — a B2B workflow automation SaaS — was preparing for their public launch after 8 months in private beta. They had a waitlist of 3,200 people, a modest ad budget of $8,000, and a hard deadline: 7 days to produce a full launch video suite. Their previous agency had delivered a generic explainer video that the founder described as "something you'd see on a stock footage website."

They needed three things: a hero launch video for the homepage, a 60-second ad for Meta and LinkedIn, and a product walkthrough that could live on YouTube and in their onboarding flow. All three needed to feel premium, move fast, and make the product look like the obvious choice in a crowded market.

Hammad wrote all three scripts in 48 hours. Mehmood handled the full edit with custom motion graphics. Shah directed the overall campaign narrative. We delivered on day 6.

The Process

Three Videos.
One Narrative.

01

Positioning Sprint

Shah ran a 4-hour positioning session with the Flowmatic founder. We mapped their ICP (operations managers at 10–50 person companies), identified the core pain (too many tools, too much manual work), and found the emotional hook: "You didn't hire a team to do admin. Neither did we." That line became the campaign's central message.

02

Three-Script Architecture

Hammad wrote each script for a different job. The hero video: emotional, problem-first, builds to a reveal. The ad: pure pattern interrupt — opens mid-workflow chaos, cuts to calm, ends with a single CTA. The walkthrough: educational, confidence-building, designed to reduce churn by showing users exactly what they're getting before they sign up.

03

Motion-Heavy Production

Mehmood built a custom motion graphics system for the product UI — animated screen recordings with highlight callouts, smooth transitions between features, and a kinetic type system that made data points feel exciting. The hero video used a split-screen technique showing "before Flowmatic" vs. "after Flowmatic" that became the most-shared clip from the launch.

04

Launch Sequencing

Shah mapped the 7-day launch sequence: teaser clip on day 1, hero video on day 3, ad campaign live on day 4, walkthrough published on day 5. Each piece fed into the next. The teaser drove 400 waitlist additions before the product even launched. By day 7, Flowmatic had 1,800 new signups and a pipeline the founder called "the best week in company history."
