The Brief

A great podcast.
Invisible on YouTube.

The Leverage Lab — a weekly business and entrepreneurship podcast hosted by Kamran Javed — had been running for 3 years. Strong Spotify numbers, loyal listeners, great guests. But their YouTube channel had 1,200 subscribers and their videos were raw, unedited audio recordings with a static image. They were leaving an enormous audience on the table.

Kamran came to us with a clear goal: turn the podcast into a proper YouTube show without changing what made it great. No over-production, no fake energy — just the real conversations, made watchable. We built a full production pipeline that processes each episode into a full-length YouTube video, 4–6 highlight clips, and 2 YouTube Shorts. All delivered within 48 hours of recording.

Mehmood and Ahmar split the workload: Mehmood handles the full-length episode edits, Ahmar cuts the clips and Shorts. Khizar writes the titles, descriptions, and chapter markers. Shah manages the upload strategy and thumbnail testing.

The Process

One Recording.
Ten Pieces of Content.

01

Full Episode Production

Mehmood built a signature visual style for the show: a clean two-camera split with dynamic switching, lower thirds for guest names and titles, chapter cards at key topic transitions, and a branded intro/outro sequence. The editing removes dead air and filler without making the conversation feel choppy. Average episode length went from 68 minutes to a tighter 52 minutes — same content, better pacing.

02

Clip Engineering

Ahmar watches every episode and identifies the 4–6 moments with the highest standalone value — a counterintuitive insight, a strong story, a quotable line. Each clip is 60–90 seconds, reformatted for vertical viewing, with captions and a hook card at the start. These clips drive 40% of the channel's total views and are the primary subscriber acquisition tool.

03

SEO & Discovery

Khizar writes every title, description, and chapter marker with search intent in mind. We identified that business podcast listeners on YouTube search differently than Spotify listeners — they search for specific topics, not show names. Titles like "Why Most Founders Fail at Delegation (and How to Fix It)" outperform "The Leverage Lab Ep. 47" by 8x in click-through rate. This single change doubled organic discovery in the first month.

04

Thumbnail System

Shah built a thumbnail system that tests two variants per episode: one guest-focused (face + bold text), one topic-focused (graphic + headline). After 8 weeks of data, we found that topic-focused thumbnails outperformed guest-focused by 2.3x for new viewers — but guest-focused performed better for returning subscribers. We now use both strategically based on the episode's primary growth goal.
