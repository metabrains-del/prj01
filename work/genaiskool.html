The Process

How We Built It

01

Channel Architecture

Before a single video was scripted, Shah spent a week mapping the AI education landscape on YouTube. We identified the content gaps — most channels were either too technical (alienating beginners) or too surface-level (no real value). GenAISkool's lane: practical, actionable, beginner-friendly AI tutorials with a strong income-generation angle. That positioning became the foundation for every content decision.

02

Script System

Khizar developed a repeatable script framework for the channel: a curiosity-gap hook in the first 15 seconds, a clear value promise by 30 seconds, structured teaching segments with pattern interrupts every 90 seconds, and a soft CTA woven naturally into the final third. Every script goes through two rounds of review before recording. The result: an average audience retention of 58% on videos over 10 minutes — well above the platform average.

03

Visual Identity & Editing

Mehmood built the channel's visual language from scratch: a dark, premium color palette with cyan and purple accents, kinetic text animations for key points, custom lower thirds, and a signature intro sequence that plays in the first 5 seconds of every video. The editing style is fast but not chaotic — cuts every 3–4 seconds, B-roll layered over screen recordings, and a sound design system that makes even tutorial content feel cinematic.

04

SEO & Distribution

Shah handles the full distribution strategy for every upload: title testing (we write 5 variants and pick based on CTR prediction), thumbnail A/B testing, description SEO with timestamped chapters, pinned comment strategy, and a Shorts repurposing pipeline where Ahmar cuts 3–4 clips from every long-form video. The Shorts feed has become a major subscriber acquisition channel — driving 30% of new subs in the last 6 months.
