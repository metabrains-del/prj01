The Brief

A brand with a story.
No one was telling it.

The client came to us with a product they believed in, a growing audience, and zero content that actually communicated their "why." Their existing videos were generic, forgettable, and converting at under 1%. They needed something that felt real — not polished corporate fluff, but a genuine story that made people feel something.

Shah Nawaz led the creative strategy. The brief was simple: make a 12–15 minute documentary-style video that tells the brand's origin story, builds emotional connection, and ends with a clear, non-pushy call to action. The kind of video people share because they want to, not because they're asked to.

The Process

How We Built It

01

Discovery & Research

We spent 2 days deep-diving into the brand — their origin, their founder's story, customer reviews, competitor content, and what was already working in their niche. We identified the emotional core: a founder who built something out of personal necessity, not business opportunity.

02

Script Architecture

Khizar wrote a 3-act script structure: Hook (the problem, viscerally felt), Journey (the founder's story, raw and honest), Resolution (the product as the natural answer). Every line was written to be spoken, not read — with natural pauses, emotional beats, and a hook in the first 8 seconds that held 94% of viewers past the intro.

03

Post-Production

Mehmood handled the full edit — cinematic color grade, custom motion graphics, licensed music that matched the emotional arc, and a sound design layer that made the whole thing feel like a short film. Three rounds of revisions, zero compromises on quality.

04

Launch Strategy

Shah built the launch strategy: optimal publish time, thumbnail A/B test (we ran 3 variants), title optimization for search + click-through, and a community post sequence to seed early engagement. The video hit 100K views in 48 hours — entirely organic.
