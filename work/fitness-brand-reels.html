The Brief

Great product.
Zero content presence.

IronRoot — a clean-label fitness supplement brand — had been operating for two years with a loyal but small customer base. Their products were genuinely excellent: third-party tested, no fillers, transparent labeling. But their Instagram had 4,200 followers, their reels averaged 800 views, and their content looked like every other supplement brand: generic gym footage, stock photos, and captions that read like a nutrition label.

The founder came to us with a clear frustration: "Our product is better than the brands with 500K followers. Why does their content work and ours doesn't?" The answer was simple — they were making content about their product instead of content for their audience. We fixed that.

Ayesha led the brand voice rebuild. Mohsin owned the visual identity. Shah built the 90-day content calendar. We produced 24 reels, 8 long-form YouTube videos, and a complete brand voice guide that the client's team now uses for every piece of content they create in-house.

The Process

Rebuilding a Brand
From the Inside Out

01

Audience & Competitor Audit

Ayesha spent a week inside the fitness supplement space — analyzing 300+ pieces of content, reading comment sections, mapping what the audience actually cared about vs. what brands were posting. The insight: the audience didn't want to be sold to. They wanted to be educated, entertained, and made to feel like insiders. IronRoot's content was doing none of that.

02

Brand Voice Rebuild

Ayesha wrote a complete brand voice guide: tone (direct, knowledgeable, never preachy), vocabulary (what words to use and avoid), content pillars (education, transparency, community, performance), and a hook formula specific to the fitness niche. Every script she wrote after that felt like it came from a person, not a brand — and the comments started reflecting that immediately.

03

Visual Identity Overhaul

Mohsin built a new visual system for the brand: a dark, high-contrast color palette that stood out in the feed, a custom text animation style for key claims, and a signature editing rhythm — fast cuts on energy moments, slow holds on product shots. The first reel under the new system hit 180K views. The brand's previous best was 12K.

04

90-Day Content Engine

Shah built a 90-day content calendar with 3 content types rotating weekly: educational (ingredient deep-dives, myth-busting), social proof (customer results, before/after stories), and entertainment (founder content, behind-the-scenes). The cadence was 3 reels per week plus 2 YouTube videos per month. By week 6, the brand's organic reach had grown 14x from where it started.
